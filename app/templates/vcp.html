📖 VCP Pattern Guide (Minervini)

🔥 Breaking Out

Price closed above the pivot (high of last contraction) on volume ≥ 40% above average. This is the confirmed entry — buy now or on the first pullback to the breakout level.

⚡ Near Pivot

Price within 10% of the pivot. Pattern is complete or nearly so — set an alert at the pivot price. Enter only if volume surges (≥40% above 50d avg) on the breakout day.

🔄 Forming

VCP structure detected but price is still more than 10% below the pivot. Watch for the next contraction to tighten further. Add to watchlist; do not buy until Near Pivot + breakout.

T = number of contractions. Each contraction depth must be shallower than the last (e.g. 25% → 15% → 8% → 4%). Volume must dry up at each trough. Pivot = high of last (tightest) contraction. Stage 2 trend template: ≥5 of 6 Minervini conditions required.
