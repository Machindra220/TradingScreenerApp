💡 US HH-HL Screener Strategy Guide

Validated against Weinstein Stage Analysis, Minervini SEPA, and S&P RS momentum frameworks.

1. ±10% 200-SMA Zone — Early Entry Logic

Stocks within ±10% of their 200-SMA are in the Stage 1→2 transition zone. Buying as price reclaims the 200-SMA while forming a Higher Low captures the earliest low-risk entry before the breakout is universally recognized. Stocks 0–5% above SMA (EMA Dist green) offer the best R-multiple setups in US markets.

2. 🔥 Fresh Pullback — Timing the Entry

The FRESH tag marks stocks where the pivot low formed within the last 10 bars with SL ≤ 8%. This is the Minervini VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) timing signal — the stock has pulled back to a tight base just above a confirmed HL. The sooner after the pivot you enter, the lower the risk and the higher the potential reward.

3. S&P RS Ratio — Relative Strength vs Benchmark

RS Ratio = (Stock Price / S&P 500) × 100. A ratio above 100 means the stock is outperforming the S&P 500. The 20-day RS change filters for acceleration — stocks with rising RS are expanding their edge. RS ≥ 110 with Accelerating trend identifies the strongest momentum leaders worth focusing on.

4. Stop Loss — SL % and Pivot Placement

Place stops just below the last confirmed pivot low. SL % shows this distance as % of current price. Target SL ≤ 8% (amber) for standard entries, ≤ 5% (green) for tight bases offering superior R-multiples. The Bars Since Pivot column tells you how fresh the pivot is — entries within 5 bars (green) are highest priority.

5. 52W Retracement — Avoid Extended Stocks

52W Retracement shows how far below the 52-week high the stock is trading. Combined with ±10% EMA filter, stocks with retracement ≤ 15% are near highs and have strong momentum. Stocks heavily off highs (>25%) may be forming bases but require more time — wait for a confirmed HL closer to the 200-SMA.

6. 🌱 Early Risers — Composite Filter

Early Risers combines: EMA Dist 0–+8%, Fresh pivot (≤10 bars), Accelerating RS, SL ≤ 8%, Retracement ≤ 20%, HH confirmed. This multi-factor screen identifies US stocks in the earliest Phase of Stage 2 with maximum upside and defined risk. These are the setups to size into and pyramid as they prove out.
